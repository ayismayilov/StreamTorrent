# Third-party components

StreamTorrent uses Electron (MIT), React (MIT), WebTorrent (MIT), parse-torrent (MIT), and their transitive dependencies. Packaged dependency directories retain their license files inside app.asar. Figma SVG assets were exported from the user-supplied design.

FFmpeg and FFprobe are bundled as separate executables for media probing and preview generation. These binaries come from the npm @ffmpeg-installer and @ffprobe-installer platform packages. Exact versions, tarball URLs, integrity hashes and upstream homes are recorded in vendor/SOURCES.json (also shipped beside the media executables). FFmpeg is an external executable rather than a library linked into the application. Its source, licensing and build information are available at https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html; each executable prints its build configuration with -version and its applicable license with -L. Individual builds can include GPL-enabled components. Review the corresponding source and license obligations before public redistribution.
